"Who's who in my zoo?"

Most tools make you look things up.
WhosWhoZoo already knows.

It loads everything you've saved about people and answers from that — instantly and completely. Like talking to your own memory.

Your Three Memory Tiers

🖥️Active — your desk
Four files always in front of Claude: people.md, reflections.md, fragments.md, loops.md. Every chat loads these automatically. This is your working memory — keep it lean and current.
📦Archive — your filing cabinet
Older records you don't need every day but still want accessible. Tap 📦 Archive once to include archive files in chat and search. Use /archive-review to let AI suggest what's ready to move here.
🔐Cold Storage — rarely accessed, never auto-loaded
Records you want to keep long-term but rarely need. Cold storage files are never suggested for cleanup and are never loaded automatically — they only open when you ask. Use /deep [name] to move a record here. Run /recall [name] to search cold storage, or bare /recall to browse everything in it.
Moving records between tiers
Active → Archive: /archive [name] or /archive-review
Archive → Cold Storage: /deep [name] or /deep-review
Any tier → Active: /restore [name]

How It Works

💾Your memory, your files
Your notes live in your own account — not on WhosWhoZoo servers. You can export or delete everything anytime. There are 4 active files (people, reflections, fragments, loops), 4 archive files for older records, and 4 cold storage files for rarely accessed long-term records.
🧠Claude reads everything, every time
Every question loads your entire active memory into Claude before answering — the whole file, not a search result. No guessing at which notes matter, and your archive is one toggle away when you need it, which is why it can make connections across people and topics.
📦Archive keeps it lean
Records you no longer need daily move to archive. Use the 📦 toggle to include archive files in chat and search. Cold storage is separate — use /recall to search it. Cold storage records are never loaded automatically.
🔒Curated memory — no outside noise
Claude reads only what you've saved — not the internet, not anyone else's data. That's why answers stay grounded in your own notes instead of generic web knowledge. One practical tip: when two people share a first name, or you're unsure of a spelling, give the full name. A partial or misspelled name is the one case where it can land on the wrong record, or say it can't find someone who is in fact there — if that happens, reset the context and ask again with the full name.
🎙Voice in, voice out
Tap the mic to enter conversation mode. Speak naturally. The app transcribes, sends to Claude, and reads the response aloud — with URLs and code replaced by verbal cues so it stays listenable.

Privacy & Cost

🛡️Your data never trains AI models
WhosWhoZoo uses the Anthropic API — not the Claude.ai website. API usage is explicitly excluded from model training under Anthropic's terms. Your notes are processed by Claude to answer your questions, but are never stored by Anthropic long-term or used to improve any AI model. If you use voice replies, the spoken text passes through ElevenLabs or OpenAI TTS — also under API terms that prohibit training use.
☁️Stored at the edge, not a central server
Your memory files are stored on Cloudflare's global network — the same infrastructure that powers millions of websites. There is no WhosWhoZoo database, no account to breach. Your data lives in your Cloudflare account, and inside the app your passphrase is what unlocks it. Worth knowing: the Cloudflare API token you used at setup can read those files directly, without your passphrase — which is exactly why we recommend deleting it once you're installed. Photos (if enabled) are stored in Cloudflare R2 object storage — free tier is 10 GB/month, though a credit card on file is required to activate R2 even at no cost.
💳No subscription — fill a bucket, use what you need
You add credits to your own Anthropic and OpenAI accounts (like prepaid balances) and pay only for what you use. That's a few dollars a month starting out, and it grows as your memory does — every question re-sends everything you've written, so a large, long-used memory costs more per question than a small one. Type /costs any time to see your running total, and set a daily cap in Settings. Voice replies use either OpenAI TTS (Marin & Onyx voices, pay-as-you-go from the same OpenAI bucket) or ElevenLabs (Brittney & Callum voices — also pay-as-you-go; a few dollars of ElevenLabs credit covers normal use). No required subscription — for any of it.

Memory Audit

The audit looks for integrity issues in your memory — broken characters in metadata, malformed headers, date inconsistencies — separately from the size-based memory lifecycle nudges.

/audit runs the free Quick scan over your active and archived files. /audit deep adds near-duplicate and name-mismatch detection, plus photo integrity checks — broken references (photo listed in a record but missing from storage) and orphaned files (in storage but not referenced anywhere). Cost confirmed before it runs. /audit ignored lists findings you've dismissed and lets you un-ignore individual entries.

Every finding has two actions: Open (jumps to the record's edit modal so you can fix it) and Ignore (suppresses that exact issue in future audits).

Voice Input Modes

Two transcription engines are available. Switch between them in Settings → Voice input. The setting persists across sessions.

⚡Auto — fast, free, browser-native
Uses the browser's built-in Web Speech Recognition API. Instant — transcription appears as you speak. No API credits used. Accuracy depends on your browser and accent. Best for quick questions and most everyday use. On iOS, Auto always falls back to Whisper because iOS Safari does not support Web Speech API.
🎙Whisper — accurate, small cost, slight delay
Sends audio to OpenAI's Whisper-1 model via your worker's /transcribe endpoint. More accurate than Auto — especially for names, technical terms, and quieter environments. Costs a small fraction of a cent per clip (billed to your OpenAI account). Adds a 1–3 second round-trip while the audio is processed. Requires your OpenAI API key to be configured.
🌐Voice Language — Whisper only
Settings → Voice Language. Sets the language Whisper listens for: English, हिन्दी (Hindi), Español, or Français. Defaults to English. Pinning the language prevents Whisper from hallucinating text on near-silent audio — a known Whisper quirk when no language is specified. Only applies to Whisper mode; Auto uses your browser's speech recognition which detects language on its own.
💾Token Saver — Settings → Token Saver
Controls what happens to the conversation when you close voice mode. On (default): the conversation history is cleared on exit — frees up context tokens so the next session starts fresh. Off: conversation history is preserved — you can continue the chat exactly where you left off after closing voice mode. Turn it off if you want to carry context across voice sessions.

Tips

Just ask naturally. You don't need commands for most things. "Who is Adam?" or "what do I know about the PTMM project?" work as plain questions.
Single words trigger tag search. Type a single word or phrase with no verb and the app searches all records for that tag exhaustively before answering.
Add a file scope to any search. /edit people adam narrows to people.md. /archive loops archives from loops.md only.
Open loops surface automatically on login. Each time you open the app, a 📋 Open loops card appears if any loops.md records are overdue, due today, due this week, have unchecked tasks, or were created in the last 7 days. Tap any row to open that loop in the editor.
Archive keeps Claude fast. Move stale records to archive. They're still searchable with the 📦 Archive toggle on — just not loaded in every chat message.
Voice skips URLs and code. In conversation mode, links say "see link in chat" and code says "see code in chat" — the full content is still in the chat bubble.
/r is your reset valve. If a conversation goes sideways, /r clears context without losing your memory files.
Bring cold storage records into chat. Use /recall [name] to find a cold storage record, then tap "Use in chat" — a purple bar appears above the input confirming the record is loaded. Claude will see it in every message until you tap clear.
Memory files are Markdown. When editing records directly with /edit, headers use ##, bullets use -, and bold uses **text**. Not sure about the syntax? Markdown basic syntax →
Auto-lock preserves your conversation. If the app locks due to inactivity, your conversation history is intact. Unlock to continue exactly where you left off — Claude still has full context. Only a manual sign-out clears the conversation.
Install to your home screen for a native-app feel. The app works as a full-screen installed app on both platforms — no App Store required.

Chrome, Edge, or Samsung Internet: after you log in, look for a one-tap Install banner above the message box — tap it and you're done. Doesn't appear on Safari/iPhone (no such option exists there) or if the app is already installed.

Android (manual): Open your app URL in Chrome → tap the three-dot menu → Add to Home Screen.
iPhone: Open your app URL in Safari (not Chrome) → tap the Share button (square with arrow) → Add to Home Screen.

Once installed, the app launches without browser chrome. Biometric unlock (fingerprint or Face ID) may also become available if it wasn't working in the browser tab.
